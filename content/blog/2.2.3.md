We're announcing the release of OpenFusionLauncher v2.2.3.

This update introduces a critical fix for a use-after-free bug that could cause the game to crash on launch. The launcher will also double check that the main.unity3d is available over HTTPS before upgrading the request.

### Changelog

Full changelog available [here](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/compare/2.2.2...2.2.3).

## Download
The launcher can be grabbed from our [Downloads Page](https://openfusion.dev/download) or the [GitHub Release Page](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/releases/tag/2.2.3).
