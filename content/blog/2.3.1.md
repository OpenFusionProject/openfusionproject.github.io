We're announcing the release of OpenFusionLauncher v2.3.1.

This update brings a couple of important bugfixes:

- Fixes a severe resource leak when the game is launched on a computer without Discord.
- Fixes occasional texture loading failures in-game when connected to an endpoint server.
- Fixes a crash during game shutdown.

### Changelog

Full changelog available [here](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/compare/2.3.0...2.3.1).

## Download
The launcher can be grabbed from our [Downloads Page](https://openfusion.dev/download) or the [GitHub Release Page](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/releases/tag/2.3.1).
