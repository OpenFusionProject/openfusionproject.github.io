We're announcing the release of OpenFusionLauncher v2.2.1.

This patch fixes an issue where the game would fail to launch if any of the folders the executables are stored in have spaces.

### Changelog

Full changelog available [here](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/compare/2.2.0...2.2.1).

## Download
The launcher can be grabbed from our [Downloads Page](https://openfusion.dev/download) or the [GitHub Release Page](https://github.com/OpenFusionProject/OpenFusionLauncher/releases/tag/2.2.1).
